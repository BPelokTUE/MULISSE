\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Goal}

Our goal is to derive (approximately) optimal parameters for flat envelope indexes for normalized subsequence search, given a size constraint. Namely, we want to find a cost function $f$ such that we can write the constrained optimization problem:
$$\begin{array}{rl}
    \text{minimize} & f(\beta, \gamma, s) \\[4pt]
    \text{s.t.} & g(\beta, \gamma, s) \le \text{Size}
\end{array}$$
where $\beta$ is the number of lengths per length group, $\gamma$ is the number of starting positions per envelope, and $s$ is the segment size, and the function $g$ measures the size of the index, and is defined as:
$$g(\beta,\gamma,s) := \sum_{i=1}^{N_l}\left(\left\lceil\frac{m-l_{\max}^i+1}{\gamma}\right\rceil\sum_{c=1}^{|C|}\left(\left\lfloor\frac{l_{\max}^i}{s}\right\rfloor+o_\text{env}\right)+o_\text{lg}\right)$$
where:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $m$ is the length of the time series
    \item $|C|$ is the number of channels
    \item $l_{\min}$ and $l_{\max}$ are the minimum and maximum lengths of subsequences searched for
    \item $N_l:=\lceil(l_{\max}-l_{\min}+1)/\beta\rceil$ is the number of length groups
    \item $l_{\min}^i$ and $l_{\max}^i$ are the minimum and maximum lengths in the $i$-th length group respectively
    \item $o_\text{env}$ is the memory overhead per envelope
    \item $o_\text{lg}$ is the memory overhead per length group
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Cost Function}

Let the set of values summarized in the $k$'th segment of the $j$'th envelope of the $i$'th length group of time series $t$ from dataset $\mathcal{D}$ be defined as follows: \textbf{The mean should not be divided. This mistake was carried through the rest of the calculations, so mostly everything else is incorrect.}
$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
    \envelopeijkt & := & \left\{ \frac{\segsumplk-\subsmeanpl}{s\subsvarpl}  \mid l\in \lengthrange{i},p\in \posrange{j} \right\} \\[4pt]
    & \text{where} & \\[4pt]
    \subsmeanpl & := & \sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1} t_{i'} / l \\[4pt]
    \subsvarpl & := & \sqrt{\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}\left(t_{i'}-\subsmeanpl\right)^2/l} \\[4pt]
    \segsumplk & := & \sum_{i'=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}t_{i'}\\[4pt]
    \lengthrange{i} & := & \left[l_{\min}+(i-1)\beta,l_{\min}+i\beta\right) = [l_{\min}^i,l_{\max}^i] \\[4pt]
    \posrange{j} & := & \left[j\gamma, (j+1)\gamma\right) \\
\end{array}
$$
then the lower and upper bound of the $i,j,k$ segment are defined as:
$$\begin{array}{rcl}
    l_{i,j,k}^t & := & \min\{\envelopeijkt\} \\[4pt]
    u_{i,j,k}^t & := & \max\{\envelopeijkt\}
\end{array}$$

\textbf{How can we use these definitions to derive a reasonable cost function?} One approach would be to estimate the expected pruning power of the index based on the parameters $\beta$, $\gamma$, and $s$. To do this, let us order the envelopes in the length group of the query $Q$, denoted by index $i_Q$, in non-descending order of their distance to the query:
$$d\left(Q,E_{i_Q,j_1}^{t_1}\right)\le d\left(Q,E_{i_Q,j_2}^{t_2}\right)\le ...\le d\left(Q,E_{i_Q,j_{|E_{i_Q}|}}^{t_{|E_{i_Q}|}}\right)$$
where the distance of the query to an envelope is defined as the minimum raw distance between the query and any subsequence of length $|Q|$ in the envelope:
$$d\left(Q,E_{i_Q,j}^t\right):=\min\left\{d\left(Q,t_{p:|Q|}\right) \mid p \in [j\gamma,(j+1)\gamma) \right\}$$
additionally, the min-distance of any envelope to the query is defined as:
$$d_{\min}\left(Q,E_{i_Q,j}^t\right):=\sum_{k=1}^{\lfloor |Q| / s \rfloor}\begin{cases}
    \left(\hat Q_k - u_{i_Q,j,k}^t\right)^2s & \text{if } \hat Q_k > u_{i_Q,j,k}^t \\[4pt]
    \left(\hat Q_k - l_{i_Q,j,k}^t\right)^2s & \text{if } \hat Q_k < l_{i_Q,j,k}^t \\[4pt]
    0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}$$

\textbf{Observation}: The fewer min-distances are smaller than the maximum distance of the exact result set to the query (or $R$ in case of $R$-range queries), the higher the pruning power of the index. Therefore, to increase the pruning power, we can \textit{bring the min-distances closer to the actual distances}, i.e. we can:
$$\underset{\beta,\gamma,s}{\text{minimize}}\quad \mathbb{E}\left[d\left(Q,E_{i_Q,j'}^t\right)-d_{\min}\left(Q,E_{i_Q,j'}^t\right)\right]$$

This can be achieved by increasing the expected lower bounds and decreasing the expected upper bounds, i.e. \textit{by decreasing the variance of the envelope segments}:

$$\underset{\beta,\gamma,s}{\text{minimize}}\quad\mathbb{V}\left[E_{i,j,k}^t\right]$$

\textit{Note}: This objective does not take into consideration the $s$ in the summation of segment distances in $d_{\min}$. This may be incorporated in the objective later on.

\section{Closed Form}

We wish to find the closed form of $\mathbb{V}\left[\envelopeijkt\right]$ so that we can minimize it. The close form of the variance should be a function $h(l,p,k,s)$, which we can average over $l\in[1,...,N_l]$, $p\in[1,...,N_p]$ and $k\in[1,...,N_s^l]$ to get $f(\beta,\gamma,s)$. We may assume the following:

$$
\begin{array}{rcll}
    t_{i'} & = & \begin{cases}
        0 & \text{if } i' = 0 \\
        t_{i'-1}+N(0,1) & \text{if } i' > 0
    \end{cases} & \text{for } i'\in\mathcal{D} \\[4pt]
    & \text{and} & \\[4pt]
    \expectation{\frac{X}{Y}} & \approx & \frac{\expectation{X}}{\expectation{Y}} - \frac{\covariance{X}{Y}}{\expectation{Y}^2} + \frac{\variance{Y}\expectation{X}}{\expectation{Y}^3} & \text{for r.v.s } X,Y
\end{array}
$$

\subsection{Derivation}

$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
    \variance{\envelopeijkt} & = & \expectation{\left(\frac{\segsumplk-\subsmeanpl}{s\subsvarpl}-\expectation{\envelopeijkt}\right)^2} \\[16pt]
    \expectation{\envelopeijkt} & = & \expectation{\frac{\segsumplk}{s\subsvarpl}} - \expectation{\frac{\subsmeanpl}{s\subsvarpl}} \\[16pt]
\end{array}
$$
the expectation $\expectation{\frac{\segsumplk}{s\subsvarpl}}$ is $0$, because the joint distribution of $\segsumplk$ and $\subsvarpl$ is symmetric around $0$, since $\segsumplk$ is normally distributed with mean $0$ and $\subsvarpl$ is invariant under sign change of $t_{i'}$. Therefore the joint distribution, denoted as $p_{S,\sigma}$ is an odd function, leading to:
$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
    \expectation{\frac{\segsumplk}{s\subsvarpl}} & = & \iint_{\mathbb{R}^2}\frac{x}{y}p_{S,\sigma}(x,y)dxdy \\[8pt]
    & = & \iint_{\mathbb{R}^2}\frac{-x}{y}p_{S,\sigma}(-x,y)dxdy \\[8pt]
    & = & -\iint_{\mathbb{R}^2}\frac{x}{y}p_{S,\sigma}(x,y)dxdy = -\expectation{\frac{\segsumplk}{s\subsvarpl}} \\[12pt]
    & \Rightarrow & \expectation{\frac{\segsumplk}{s\subsvarpl}} \overset{(1)}{=} 0
\end{array}
$$

Following a similar reasoning, we can show that $\mathbb{E}\left[\frac{\mu_{p:l}^t}{s\sigma_{p:l}^t}\right]=0$ and as a consequence $\mathbb{E}[E_{i,j,k}]=0$. Therefore we can write:
$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
    \variance{\envelopeijkt} & = & \expectation{\left(\frac{\segsumplk-\subsmeanpl}{s\subsvarpl}\right)^2} \\[20pt]
    & \approx & \underbrace{\frac{\expectation{(\segsumplk-\subsmeanpl)^2}}{\expectation{(s\subsvarpl)^2}}}_{A} - \underbrace{\frac{\covariance{(\segsumplk-\subsmeanpl)^2}{(s\subsvarpl)^2}}{\expectation{(s\subsvarpl)^2}^2}}_{B} + \underbrace{\frac{\variance{(s\subsvarpl)^2}\expectation{(\segsumplk-\subsmeanpl)^2}}{\expectation{(s\subsvarpl)^2}^3}}_{C}
\end{array}
$$

We start by calculating the denominator:

$$\begin{array}{rcl}
    \expectation{(s\subsvarpl)^2} & = & \expectation{\left(s\sqrt{\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}\left(t_{i'}-\subsmeanpl\right)^2/l}\right)^2} \\[16pt]
    & = & \frac{s^2}{l}\expectation{\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}\left(t_{i'}-\subsmeanpl\right)^2} \\[16pt] 
    & = & \frac{s^2}{l}\expectation{\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}\left(t_{i'}^2-2t_{i'}\subsmeanpl+(\subsmeanpl)^2\right)} \\[16pt]
    & = & \frac{s^2}{l}\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}\left(\expectation{t_{i'}^2-2t_{i'}\sum_{j'=p}^{p+l-1}\frac{t_{j'}}{l}+\sum_{j'=p}^{p+l-1}\sum_{j''=p}^{p+l-1}\frac{t_{j'}t_{j''}}{l^2}}\right) \\[16pt]
\end{array}$$
$$\begin{array}{rcl}
    & = & \frac{s^2}{l}\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}\left(\expectation{t_{i'}^2}-\frac{2}{l}\sum_{j'=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{t_{i'}t_{j'}}+\frac{1}{l^2}\sum_{j'=p}^{p+l-1}\sum_{j''=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{t_{j'}t_{j''}}\right) \\[16pt]
\end{array}$$
for $\expectation{t_{i'}t_{j'}}$ we have the following cases:
$$\expectation{t_{i'}t_{j'}}=\begin{cases}
    \expectation{t_{i'}^2} = i' & \text{if } i' = j' \\[4pt]
    \expectation{t_{i'}(t_{i'}+N(0,j'-i'))} = \expectation{t_{i'}^2} + \expectation{t_{i'}}\expectation{N(0,j'-i')} = i' & \text{if } i' < j' \\[4pt]
    \expectation{(t_{j'}+N(0,i'-j'))t_{j'}} = \expectation{t_{j'}^2} + \expectation{N(0,i'-j')}\expectation{t_{j'}} = j' & \text{if } i' > j'
\end{cases}$$
so in general, we can write:
$$\expectation{t_{i'}t_{j'}}=\min(i',j')$$
and therefore:
$$\begin{array}{rcl}
    \expectation{(s\subsvarpl)^2} & = & \frac{s^2}{l}\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}\left(i'-\frac{2}{l}\sum_{j'=p}^{p+l-1}\min(i',j')+\frac{1}{l^2}\sum_{j'=p}^{p+l-1}\sum_{j''=p}^{p+l-1}\min(j',j'')\right)
\end{array}$$
the inner sums evaluate to:
$$\begin{array}{rcl}
    \sum_{j'=p}^{p+l-1}\min(i',j') & = & \sum_{j'=p}^{i'-1}j' + \sum_{j'=i'}^{p+l-1}i' \\[8pt]
    & = & \frac{(i'-1)i'}{2} - \frac{(p-1)p}{2} + (p+l-i')i' \\[8pt]
    & = & \dfrac{1}{2}\left(-i'^2-i'-p^2+p+2i'p+2i'l\right)
\end{array}$$
and:
$$\begin{array}{rcl}
    \sum_{j'=p}^{p+l-1}\sum_{j''=p}^{p+l-1}\min(j',j'') & = & \sum_{j'=p}^{p+l-1}\frac{-j'^2-j'-p^2+p+2j'p+2j'l}{2} \\[8pt]
    & = & \frac{-1}{2}\sum_{j'=p}^{p+l-1}j'^2 + \frac{2p+2l-1}{2}\sum_{j'=p}^{p+l-1}j' - \frac{p^2-p}{2}l \\[8pt]
    & = & \frac{(p-1)p(2p-1)-(p+l-1)(p+l)(2p+2l-1)}{12} \\[8pt]
    && + \frac{(2p+2l-1)((p+l-1)(p+l)-(p-1)p)}{4} - \frac{p^2l-pl}{2} \\[8pt]
    & = & \frac{l}{6} \left(2 l^{2} + 6l p - 3 l + 1\right)
\end{array}$$
plugging these results into the denominator, we get:
$$\begin{array}{rcl}
    \expectation{(s\subsvarpl)^2} & = & \frac{s^2}{l}\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}\left(i'-\frac{2}{l}\left(\frac{-i'^2-i'-p^2+p+2i'p+2i'l}{2}\right)+ \frac{1}{l^2}\left(\frac{l \left(2 l^{2} + 6 l p - 3 l +  1\right)}{6}\right)\right) \\[16pt]
    & = & ... \; = \dfrac{s^2}{6l} \left(l^{2} - 1\right)
\end{array}$$

Next we calculate $\expectation{(\segsumplk-\subsmeanpl)^2}$, which appears in both $A$ and $C$:
$$\begin{array}{rcl}
    \expectation{(\segsumplk-\subsmeanpl)^2} & = & \expectation{\left( \sum_{i'=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}t_{i'} - \frac{\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1} t_{i'}}{l} \right)^2} \\[16pt]
    & = & \expectation{\left(\sum_{i'=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}t_{i'}\right)^2}-\frac{2}{l}\expectation{\left(\sum_{i'=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}t_{i'}\right)\left(\sum_{j'=p}^{p+l-1}t_{j'}\right)}\\[16pt]
    && +\frac{1}{l^2}\expectation{\left(\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}t_{i'}\right)^2} \\[16pt]
\end{array}$$
we can reuse the result $\expectation{t_{i'}t_{j'}}=\min(i',j')$ to calculate these terms:
$$\begin{array}{rcl}
    \expectation{\left(\sum_{i'=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}t_{i'}\right)^2} & = & \sum_{i'=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\sum_{i''=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\expectation{t_{i'}t_{i''}} \\[8pt]
    & = & \sum_{i'=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\left(\sum_{i''=p+(k-1)s}^{i'-1}i'' + (p+ks-i')i'\right) \\[8pt]
    & = & \dfrac{s}{6} \left(6 k s^{2} + 6 p s - 4 s^{2} - 3 s + 1\right) \\[16pt]
    \expectation{\left(\sum_{i'=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}t_{i'}\right)\left(\sum_{j'=p}^{p+l-1}t_{j'}\right)} & = & \sum_{i'=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\sum_{j'=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{t_{i'}t_{j'}} \\[8pt]
    & = & \sum_{i'=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\left(\sum_{j'=p}^{i'-1}j' + \sum_{j'=i'}^{p+l-1}i'\right) \\[8pt]
    & = & \dfrac{s}{6} \left(- 3 k^{2} s^{2} + 6 k l s + 3 k s^{2} + 6 l p - 3 l s - 3 l - s^{2} + 1\right) \\[16pt]
    \expectation{\left(\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}t_{i'}\right)^2} & = & \sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}\sum_{i''=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{t_{i'}t_{i''}} \\[8pt]
    & = & \dfrac{l}{6} \left(2 l^{2} + 6 l p - 3 l + 1\right)
\end{array}$$
plugging these results back into the expectation, we get:
$$\begin{array}{rcl}
    \expectation{(\segsumplk-\subsmeanpl)^2} & = & \dfrac{6k^{2} s^{3} - 6k s^{3} + 2s^{3} - 2s + 1}{6l} \\[12pt]
    && + \dfrac{6 k s^{3} - 12 k s^{2} + 2 l + 6 p s^{2} - 12 p s + 6 p - 4 s^{3} + 3 s^{2} + 7 s - 3}{6}\\
\end{array}$$

Next we calculate the covariance term in $B$:
$$\begin{array}{rcl}
    \covariance{(\segsumplk-\subsmeanpl)^2}{(s\subsvarpl)^2} & = & \expectation{(\segsumplk-\subsmeanpl)^2(s\subsvarpl)^2} \\[8pt]
    && - \expectation{(\segsumplk-\subsmeanpl)^2}\expectation{(s\subsvarpl)^2} \\[16pt]
\end{array}$$

We only need to calculate the first term:
$$\begin{array}{rcl}
    \expectation{(\segsumplk-\subsmeanpl)^2(s\subsvarpl)^2} & = & \mathbb{E}\left[\left(\sum_{i'=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}t_{i'} - \dfrac{\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1} t_{i'}}{l}\right)^2\right. \\[16pt]
    && \left. \dfrac{s^2}{l}\left(\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}\left(t_{i'}-\dfrac{\sum_{i''=p}^{p+l-1} t_{i''}}{l}\right)^2\right) \right]   
\end{array}$$
$$\begin{array}{rcl}
    & = & \mathbb{E}\left[ \left( \left(\sum_{i'=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}t_{i'}\right)^2 - \frac{2}{l}\sum_{i'=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\sum_{i''=p}^{p+l-1}t_{i'}t_{i''}+\frac{1}{l^2}\left(\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}t_{i'}\right)^2\right) \right. \\[16pt]
    && \left. \frac{s^2}{l}\left(\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}t_{i'}^2 - \frac{2}{l}\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}\sum_{i''=p}^{p+l-1}t_{i'}t_{i''} + \frac{1}{l^2}\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}\left(\sum_{i''=p}^{p+l-1}t_{i'}\right)^2\right)\right] \\ [16pt]
    & = & \frac{s^2}{l}\sum_{i_1,i_2=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\sum_{i_3=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{t_{i_1}t_{i_2}t_{i_3}^2} - \frac{2s^2}{l^2}\sum_{i_1,i_2=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\sum_{i_3,i_4=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{t_{i_1}t_{i_2}t_{i_3}t_{i_4}} \\[8pt]
    && + \frac{s^2}{l^3}\sum_{i_1,i_2=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\sum_{i_3,i_4,i_5=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{\prod_{j'=1}^5t_{i_j'}} - \frac{2s^2}{l^2}\sum_{i_1=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\sum_{i_2,i_3=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{t_{i_1}t_{i_2}t_{i_3}^2} \\[8pt]
    && + \frac{4s^2}{l^3}\sum_{i_1=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\sum_{i_2,i_3,i_4=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{t_{i_1}t_{i_2}t_{i_3}t_{i_4}} - \frac{2s^2}{l^4}\sum_{i_1=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\sum_{i_2,...,i_5=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{\prod_{j'=1}^5t_{i_{j'}}}\\[8pt]
    && + \frac{s^2}{l^3}\sum_{i_1,i_2,i_3=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{t_{i_1}t_{i_2}t_{i_3}^2} - \frac{2s^2}{l^4}\sum_{i_1,...,i_4=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{t_{i_1}t_{i_2}t_{i_3}t_{i_4}} + \frac{s^2}{l^5}\sum_{i_1,...,i_5=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{\prod_{j'=1}^5t_{i_{j'}}} \\[8pt]
    & =: & \frac{s^2}{l}B_1 -\frac{2s^2}{l^2}B_2 + \frac{s^2}{l^3}B_3 - \frac{2s^2}{l^2}B_4 +\frac{4s^2}{l^3}B_5 - \frac{2s^2}{l^4}B_6 + \frac{s^2}{l^3}B_7 - \frac{2s^2}{l^4}B_8+ \frac{s^2}{l^5}B_9 
\end{array}$$

To calculate the $\expectation{\prod t_{i'}}$ terms, we can use Isserlis' theorem:
$$
\expectation{\prod_{j'=1}^\text{order} t_{i_{j'}}} = \sum_{\text{pairings } \pi}\prod_{(i,j)\in\pi}\expectation{t_{i}t_{j}}
$$

We can also use the following intermediate results:
$$\begin{array}{rcl}
    \sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}\sum_{j'=p}^{p+l-1}\min(i',j') & = & \underbrace{\dfrac{l}{6}\left(2 l^{2} + 6 l p - 3 l + 6 p^{2} - 6 p + 1\right)}_{=:\min_{pp}} \\[12pt]
    \sum_{i'=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\sum_{j'=p}^{p+l-1}\min(i',j') & = & \underbrace{\dfrac{s}{6}\left(- 3 k^{2} s^{2} + 6 k l s + 3 k s^{2} + 6 l p - 3 l s - 3 l - s^{2} + 1\right)}_{=:\min_{pk}} \\[12pt]
    \sum_{i'=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\sum_{j'=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\min(i',j') & = & \sum_{i'=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\left( \sum_{j'=p+(k-1)s}^{i'-1}j' + (p+ks-i')i' \right) \\[8pt]
    & = & \underbrace{\dfrac{s}{6} \left(6 k s^{2} + 6 p s - 4 s^{2} - 3 s + 1\right)}_{=:\min_{kk}}
\end{array}$$

$$\begin{array}{rcl}
    B_1 & = & \sum_{i_1=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\sum_{i_2=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\sum_{i_3=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{t_{i_1}t_{i_2}}\expectation{t_{i_3}^2} \\[8pt]
    && + 2\sum_{i_1=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\sum_{i_2=p}^{p+l-1}\sum_{i_3=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{t_{i_1}t_{i_3}}\expectation{t_{i_2}t_{i_3}} \\[8pt]
    & = & \min_{kk}\min_{p^2} + \sum_{i_1=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\sum_{i_3=p}^{p+l-1}\min(i_1,i_3)\left(-i_3^2-i_3-p^2+p+2i_3p+2i_3l\right) \\[8pt]
    & = & \min_{kk}\min_{p^2} + \sum_{i_1=p+(k-1)s}^{p+ks-1}\left(\sum_{i_3=p}^{i_1-1}i_3\left(-i_3^2-i_3-p^2+p+2i_3p+2i_3l\right)\right. \\[8pt]

Finally, we calculate the variance term in $C$:
$$\begin{array}{rcl}
    \variance{\left(s\subsvarpl\right)^2} & = & \expectation{\left(s\subsvarpl\right)^4} - \expectation{\left(s\subsvarpl\right)^2}^2 \\[8pt]
    \expectation{\left(s\subsvarpl\right)^4} & = & \expectation{\left(s\sqrt{\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}\left(t_{i'}-\subsmeanpl\right)^2/l}\right)^4} \\[16pt]
    & = & \frac{s^4}{l^2}\expectation{\left(\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}\left(t_{i'}-\sum_{j'=p}^{p+l-1}t_{j'}/l\right)^2\right)^2} \\[16pt]
    & = & \frac{s^4}{l^2}\expectation{\left(\sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}\left(t_{i'}^2 - \frac{2}{l}\sum_{j'=p}^{p+l-1}t_{i'}t_{j'} + \frac{1}{l^2}\left(\sum_{j'=p}^{p+l-1}t_{j'}\right)^2\right)\right)^2} \\[16pt]
    & = & \frac{s^4}{l^2}\left(\sum_{i_1,i_2=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{t_{i_1}^2t_{i_2}^2} + \frac{4}{l^2}\sum_{i_1,...,i_4=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{t_{i_1}t_{i_2}t_{i_3}t_{i_4}} \right. \\[8pt]
    && + \frac{1}{l^4}\sum_{i_1,...,i_6=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{\prod_{i'\in\{i_1,...,i_6\}}t_{i'}} - \frac{4}{l}\sum_{i_1,i_2,i_3=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{t_{i_1}t_{i_2}t_{i_3}} \\[8pt]
    && + \left. \frac{2}{l^2}\sum_{i_1,...,i_4=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{t_{i_1}t_{i_2}t_{i_3}t_{i_4}} - \frac{4}{l^3}\sum_{i_1,...,i_5=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{\prod_{i'\in\{i_1,...,i_5\}}t_{i'}} \right) \\[8pt]
\end{array}$$

Formula for expectation of two squared terms:

$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
    \sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}\sum_{i''=p}^{p+l-1}\expectation{t_{i'}^2t_{i''}^2} & = & \sum_{i'=p}^{p+l-1}\sum_{i''=p}^{p+l-1}\left(\expectation{t_{i'}^2}\expectation{t_{i''}^2} + 2\expectation{t_{i'}t_{i''}}\expectation{t_{i'}t_{i''}} \right) \\[8pt]
